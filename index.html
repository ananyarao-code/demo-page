Discovering the technology shaping our future!

About This Course

Introduction to Artificial Intelligence is a trimester course in which students discover what artificial intelligence (AI) is, how it is currently used, and how it could be used in the future. Students will learn about machine learning, deep learning, and neural networks and how these technologies already influence decisions in everyday life. They will explore the history of AI, its current applications, and its many possible uses in the future while considering the questions and concerns surrounding this growing technology. Through hands-on activities, students will experiment with different AI applications and try basic programming of an artificially intelligent system.

What Will We Learn?

Students will explore how AI works, experiment with AI tools, discover real-world applications, and learn how artificial intelligence could shape the future. They will also discuss the benefits, limitations, and responsible use of AI.
