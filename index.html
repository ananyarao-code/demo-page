About This Course

Introduction to Artificial Intelligence is a trimester course where students explore artificial intelligence (AI), how it works, and how it is used in everyday life. Students will learn about machine learning, deep learning, and neural networks, while discovering how AI helps people make decisions and solve problems. The course will also explore the history of AI, its current applications, and its potential uses in the future. Through hands-on activities, students will experiment with different AI tools, try basic programming, and discuss important questions about the benefits, challenges, and ethical concerns surrounding this growing technology.

What Will We Learn?

Students will discover how AI works, explore real-world examples, experiment with AI applications, and learn how technology may continue to change the world. They will also consider how AI should be used responsibly.
